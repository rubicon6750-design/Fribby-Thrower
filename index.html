Mega Fribby Skill Tree

The Fribby Core sits at the center of a radial upgrade web, with ten branches growing outward, including Engineering, which unlocks a Scratch-like block-coding workbench. Each upgrade you buy on the central Ascension Path unlocks the next tier of upgrades across every outer branch. To access stronger ascension upgrades, buy the Ascension Path nodes first; outer-branch upgrades stay locked until their matching path rung is unlocked. Hover over or focus any node to inspect its effect and cost. Eternal Echo waits beyond the final rung.
